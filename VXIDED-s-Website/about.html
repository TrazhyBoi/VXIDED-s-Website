The come up

Every project is another page. Here’s where this chapter started.

  1. 01

    The first steps

    Two singles, then a bigger idea

    “savin’ grace” and “FINAL” were the first singles out. Those songs led into DO I STILL GOT TIME TO GROW? — the first EP, and still my favorite project.

  2. 02

    A new chapter

    More questions, more growth

    QUESTIONS became the next EP and another favorite. Every release is a snapshot of where I’m at creatively — not the whole story, just the part I’m ready to share.

  3. 03

    Still building

    Creating with the people around me

    The music keeps moving through collaborations, remixes, and new ideas. “demo4” with thelilbird was made as a comeback song — and the fifth track we’ve made together.
