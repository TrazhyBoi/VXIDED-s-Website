More than the music

What Music Means to Me. My Dream.

My Origins. I originally started making music in mid-late 2020, I had taken the name "AshsWRLD" and was releasing some music under that, the only notable ones that people ever remember are "NOTHIN'" and "Blue Lemonade", which Blue Lemonade had my friend at the time Shekel feature on it (We did so bad). I was still releasing some music under AshsWRLD for a good while; and I was starting to get better with my craft so I decided it was time for a change.
No more AshsWRLD, just Poserboy.

Collabing with R3NDER. After switching to Poserboy, it was already early-mid 2021, I was releasing songs like "DAY ONES", "Secret Night", and even my "first" album "the one." This is around the time I had met the artist R3NDER. We met through a Josh A & Jake Hill Facebook group, where since then we've both been making music together. Throughout 2021, we created a rap duo called "DARK KNIGHTS", we would go on to release our albums "Better Days", "MISFORTUNE", "UNDEAD", and later 2021-2025 "The Tales of DARK KNIGHTS" Trilogy.

What Ended Poserboy. R3NDER and I always had some underlying beef for no reason, other than teenager drama that didn't really matter at all. We also created our first label together, "Almost MainStream", or "ALMS" for short. ALMS was our label that we tried growing big, not really signing artists but just trying to get them more push for their music, eventually the beef had happened, and I had deleted all my music, and disbanded the label. The old post about the beef is still on X .

A New Beginning. After the beef had settled and R3NDER and I come back as friends, I rebranded a third time, this time under the name "ash." This was probably my more dominant era; I was consistently dropping in 2024-2025, with my better songs as "Cupid" featuring Heartz, "SORRY NOT SORRY" with R3NDER and EPs as "Statiic Soulz' with Heartz, and "Dream World". After mid 2025 I honestly stopped making music, I couldn't write, come up with any lyrics, not even a simple flow.. So I just stopped making music in general. The passion was gone, but by late 2025, something changed. I wanted to come back, so I did so quietly.

Becoming VXIDED. In late 2025 also, I had deleted all my music under "ash.", it sparked a little bit more beef between R3NDER and I as he had to reupload all of the music we made together, but I had went under my final name change, "VXIDED". In early 2026 I made songs like "FINAL", "i don't have a name for this" featuring thelilbird, and "RASENGLOCK" featuring wubbs, probably my best songs I've made so far under the new name. I had put out 2 EPs, "QUESTIONS" and "DO I STILL GOT TIME TO GROW ?", with the cover arts coming from my friends. Mid 2026 I have collabed with R3NDER, thelilbird, wubbs, and a group of more people I love to call friends, but late 2026 I really haven't made anything.

So.. what's next for VXIDED? Maybe another album.... or 2.
